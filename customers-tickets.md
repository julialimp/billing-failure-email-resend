#### RES-1927
***Issue:** I’m not sure how to add the TXT record at Vercel. Can you tell me how?*

Hello {{user.name}},

Thank you for contacting Resend!

To add the TXT record, open your Vercel project and navigate to Settings > Domains. From there, add the TXT record generated by Resend for your domain. Once you've added the DNS record, return to the Resend dashboard to verify the domain.

Since you're using Vercel, the guide below walks through the process step by step:
- Add a new DNS record.
- Select **TXT** as the record type.
- Copy the value provided by Resend.
- Save the record and wait for DNS propagation.
- Return to Resend and verify the domain.

You can find the complete walkthrough here: [Add TXT SPF Record](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/vercel#add-txt-spf-record)

If you have any questions or run into any issues during the setup, just let me know and I'll be happy to help.

#### RES-1348
***Issue:** I’m seeing a ton of 403 errors on my account. How do I fix that?*

Hello {{user.name}},

Thanks for reaching out to Resend.

I understand you're seeing a high volume of 403 errors on your account. Since a 403 error can happen for different reasons, could you please share the following details so we can narrow down the cause?
- When did you first notice the issue?
- Which endpoint are you calling?
- What is the exact error message or error code returned in the response?
- If possible, could you provide the request ID or relevant logs?

Some common causes of 403 errors include:
- **An invalid or expired API key**: To solve this you can generate a new API Key in your [dashboard](https://resend.com/api-keys)
- **[Domain Mismatch](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/403-error-domain-mismatch)** between the verified domain in Resend and the domain used in the `from` address.
- **[Domain already registered](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/domain-already-registered)** to another Resend account. In this case we suggest checking with your team to see if someone is already using Resend.
- **[Error 1010](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/403-error-1010#403-error-with-error-code-1010)**, which can occur when the required `User-Agent` header is missing
- **[Using resend.dev Domain](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/403-error-resend-dev-domain)** to send emails to recipients other than your own address. To send emails to other recipients, please [verify your domains](https://resend.com/domains) and change the `from` address to an email using this domain.

Once you provide the additional details above, we'll be able to identify the specific cause and guide you through the next steps.

Kind regards,
<br>Julia Limp

# Escalation Message
## Subject - Possible bug: Intermittent failure sending transactional emails

### Problem Description
Customer reported that transactional emails (magic links) stopped sending for approximately four hours in production, affecting thousands of requests. According to the customer, email delivery resumed without any configuration changes on their side.

Initial review shows no recent changes to the customer's API key, verified domains, or account configuration, suggesting the issue may not be account-specific.

After investigating the issue, I was not able to identify a root cause
- Reviewed the request logs provided by the customer and confirmed a spike in failed send requests between approximately 01:00 and 05:00 UTC
- Checked statuspage and confirmed there was no scheduled maintenance
- Verified the API key used in the requests was valid and active
- Confirmed the sending domain was verified and there were no recent DNS or account configuration changes.
- Compared requests before and after the affected timeframe. Requests were successful outside of the incident window without any customer intervention
- I was unable to identify a customer configuration issue, so escalating for further investigation

### Expected Behavior
Transactional emails should be accepted and processed consistently without interruption.

### Actual Behavior
Emails stopped sending intermittently for approximately four hours, preventing thousands of authentication emails from being delivered.

### Business Impact
Issue affecting the customer's production environment and authentication flows. Customer relies on these transactional emails with magic links for users' authentication. Emails not being sent prevent users from accessing their accounts.

The issue resolved without any configuration changes on the customer's side and the they requested an RCA to understand why the initerruption occured and how to prevent similar issues from happening again. They are concerned this indicates a platform instability issue rather than customer misconfiguration.

Could you please investigate what may have caused the interruption during the reported timeframe and determine whether other customers experienced similar behavior?

### Steps to Reproduce
Unable to reproduce the issue.

Based on the customer's report:
1. Send transactional emails (magic links) through the Send Email API.
2. During the affected timeframe, emails are not sent despite no configuration changes.
3. After approximately four hours, email sending resumes without customer intervention.
